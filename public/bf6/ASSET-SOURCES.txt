- Weapons / classes / modes / maps:
  g1331/bf-manager (MIT License) — see bf-manager-LICENSE.txt

- Vehicles and gadgets:
  re-fetched as original color images from Tracker.gg's public
  Battlefield 6 image CDN. No black/white recolored variants are
  generated for these item images. Items without a Tracker.gg asset
  fall back to the bf-manager assets above.

- Devgru (NSW4) weapon icon:
  one-time download from the GameTools CDN for the missing item.

  downloaded from trackercdn.com (Tracker Network) for missing items.
